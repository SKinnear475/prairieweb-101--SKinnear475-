About

Prairie Trails Collective helps people find great hiking trails in Alberta. We list the distance and difficulty of each trail so you can pick one that fits you.

Whether you are new to hiking, or have done it for years, there is a trail here for you.

FAQ

Are the trails free?

Most trails are in public parks and are free. Some parks may charge for parking.

What should I bring?

Water, snacks, sunscreen, a jacket, and a charged phone.

Can I bring my dog?

Usually yes, but please keep your dog on a leash. Check the rules of each park.
